From support to self-reliance.

Together with schools, families and our partners in Zimbabwe, we create access to nutrition, water and education. For over 25 years, one goal has guided us: empowering people to shape their own future.

Why Zimbabwe?

Because meeting people led to a commitment.

Zimbabwe is not just another place on a project list. Doris and Hans Herz got to know the country and its people, stayed in touch and established a foundation that remains rooted in trust, personal relationships and tangible impact.

Trust grows through meeting people.

Children, parents, teachers and communities help shape our projects. We listen, plan together and stay in touch. This is how initial support grows into a lasting partnership.

Helping people help themselves

Every borehole is a step towards independence.

A borehole is not the end of a project, but the start of new possibilities. A reliable water supply lets children wash their hands, enables school kitchens and gardens, and helps communities take responsibility.

  • Water for kitchens, hygiene and everyday school life
  • Gardens as a visible first step towards self-reliance
  • Projects led by their communities

Our roots

Building on our roots.

Doris and Hans Herz founded the organisation to offer direct, straightforward help. Today, the next generation continues this work with closer personal involvement, greater transparency and a clear goal: to make outside support unnecessary in the long term.

This trust has grown over time. It comes from many years of experience in Zimbabwe, regular visits and partners whose decisions are rooted in their communities.
