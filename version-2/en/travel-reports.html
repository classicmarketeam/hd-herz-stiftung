Travel reports

Visits tell the stories behind the projects.

We visit schools, speak with families and make time for our partners. Here we share our experiences in Zimbabwe: what has changed, what is working and which questions we are still exploring.

Read the reports View projects

Transparency on the ground

Every visit informs our next decisions.

During our visits, we review what works in everyday life together with schools and partners. Their experience helps us plan the next steps and direct support to where it is needed.

2025 travel report

New ideas, new encounters, new hope.

The 2025 visit shows how small changes can make a big difference: clean water, shared responsibility and economic self-reliance. Alongside school visits, we met with Kapnek, the University of Zimbabwe, the German Embassy in Harare and partners including Dr Greg and Sue Powell.

2024 travel report

Masiyarwa, health checks and strong families.

Christina and Brigitte saw the impact of the ECD programme at schools: protein-rich porridge, regular health checks and early learning. Their visit also highlighted therapy services, families with children with disabilities and food packages.
